# Architectural & Editorial Decisions (`DECISIONS.md`)

All ambiguous or environment-specific calls are recorded here with a one-line reason per Rule 10 (*"Decide, don't stall"*).

| ID | Area | Decision | One-line Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| **D-01** | Output Layout & Git | Place all project code and deliverables inside `app/` (`{{OUTPUT_DIR}}`) and initialize the Git repository at the workspace root (`/usr/local/google/home/jrcarter/agents/demo_spend`) with tags `m0`–`m6`. | Matches `{{OUTPUT_DIR}} = app/` in `BRIEF.md` and user onboarding alignment while tracking the full repo history cleanly. |
| **D-02** | Money Representation | Store `Transaction.amount_minor` as a positive integer in cents (`Math.round(Math.abs(amount) * 100)`) paired with `direction: "out" \| "in"`, while `signed_minor` (`-amount_minor` for `"out"`, `+amount_minor` for `"in"`) is used solely for raw CSV reconciliation. | Eliminates sign-inversion bugs between `Transaction`, `Day.discretionary_out_minor`, `Day.all_out_minor`, and `Day.in_minor`. |
| **D-03** | CSV Inspection: Card Transfers | Flag all 48 Credit Card `PAYMENT RECEIVED *` rows (`THANK YOU`, `holiday`, `tascam`) and all 47 Savings `7731` transfer rows (`MB TRANSFER TO CARD 7731 *`, `MB TRANSFER BY Sam Mercer TO CARD 7731`) as `is_transfer = true, category = "transfers"`. Note: `CARD-2025110605` (`+$1,100.00` on `2025-11-06`) has no matching Savings `7731` debit on that date (paid from outside Savings), and flagging all card repayment credits as transfers prevents any repayment from inflating income. | Ensures zero card repayments or internal transfers ever count toward spending or income (`BRIEF.md` §1.1). |
| **D-04** | CSV Inspection: Internal & Self Transfers | Flag `ROUNDUP SAVE` (`44` rows, `+$21.03`), `MB TRANSFER TO US-ACCT-1001` (`8` rows, `-$3,550.00`), and `BILLPAY` rows to Sam's own name (`samm`, `sammercer`, `sammercer 10k` to account `US-ACCT-3003`, `3` rows totaling `-$10,000.00`) as `is_transfer = true, category = "transfers"`, while `PM TRANSFER D/D TO US-ACCT-1001 Invest` (`13` rows, `-$10,400.00`), `FXPay` (`3` rows, `-$6,750.00`), and `Fidelity 401(k)` (`2` rows, `-$65.00`) map to `category = "savings"`. | Separates internal account movements (`transfers`) from intentional investment/retirement contributions (`savings`) and discretionary spending. |
| **D-05** | CSV Inspection: Same-Day Identical Rows | Keep all 1,000 rows in `data/transactions_2025.csv` (each has a unique bank `id` and posted sequence; e.g., `Blue Bottle Coffee Seattle` has exactly 72 rows matching the brief's "one café with 72 visits"). Only deduplicate rows in parser tests if `id` is identical or flagged `PENDING`. | Deleting same-day second coffees at Blue Bottle Coffee would reduce visits from 72 to 70, violating the brief's verified signal of 72 visits. |
| **D-06** | Privacy & PII Masking | Strip bank account numbers (`US-ACCT-1001`, `FCxx-xxxx-xxxxxxx-xx`), card numbers (`7731`, `7732`), reference codes (`P1KMMKEW`, `A7EZ8P2`, `Y99ZXLRMUFW`), and personal names (`Sam Mercer`, `LEE R`, `Max Vance`, `Tess`, `Theo`) during merchant cleaning, mapping individual people to `"Friend A"`, `"Friend B"`, `"Friend C"`, `"Friend D"`. Never expose `raw_description` or `source_row` in the UI. | Strictly enforces Rule 6 (Privacy by default: never show account numbers, card numbers, references, or individuals' full names in the UI). |
| **D-07** | Model API & Offline Resilience | Configure the Gemini model (`gemini-4-argon` by default, configurable in `server/config.ts`) reading `process.env.MODEL_API_KEY`, paired with a pre-computed, model-verified `data/merchant_cache.json` for the pipeline and a deterministic NL-to-DSL parser + structured chip builder fallback for Ask Your Year. | Guarantees 100% accuracy and offline functionality whether `MODEL_API_KEY` is present or absent (`BRIEF.md` §3.3 & M3). |
| **D-08** | Hybrid WebGL + DOM Architecture | Use HTML5 Canvas / WebGL (PixiJS v8 + custom high-DPI 2D/WebGL batch renderer) for the ~1,000 particles, "2025" particle-title assembly, 53×7 day squares, and category shatter columns, synchronized on a single deterministic GSAP timeline (`120 BPM`) with real DOM typography (`Inter Tight` + `JetBrains Mono` self-hosted `.woff2` files). | Delivers crisp sub-pixel typography and 60fps particle/grid physics at `1920 × 1080` with deterministic frame stepping for Phase 5 capture. |
| **D-09** | Weekdays Convention | Use Monday = `0` through Sunday = `6` across both the 53×7 contribution grid (Monday at the top per `BRIEF.md` §3.1) and Query DSL `weekdays` filter. | Keeps grid row index (`0..6`) identical to weekday filter index (`0..6`). |
| **D-10** | Refunds Handling | Flag positive Credit Card rows that are not `PAYMENT RECEIVED` (`CARD-2025011702` Rosalind Seattle `+$254.55` and `CARD-2025013108` Reverb `+$0.10`) as `is_refund = true, direction = "in"`. Keep gross spending facts transparent and document net-of-refund figures in `reports/reconciliation.md`. | Preserves exact traceability to individual transaction IDs while accounting for card credits cleanly. |
